## 1. Vision

Celebrait is:

> An AI-powered platform for creating personalised celebration content.

Starting point:
- printed greeting cards

End state:
- a full celebration platform

Core mechanic:

> Upload photos → generate personalised content

## 2. Long-Term Vision (3 Years)

Celebrait becomes a platform for:

- greeting cards  
- invitations  
- posters  
- digital content  
- gifts  

All generated through AI using user-provided photos.

- Generic, templated output

- Limited personalisation  

### AI tools:

- inconsistent results  
- difficult prompting  
- unreliable outputs

### Strengths:

- working end-to-end flow  
- strong output quality  
- front + inside consistency
