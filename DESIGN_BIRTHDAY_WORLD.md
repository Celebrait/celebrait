## The house position (unchanged, now evidence-backed)

Celebrait Quirky — flat print-physics illustration, typeset-clean
lettering, per-card palettes and typefaces — stays the look. Nothing
on Thortful's walls looks like it: their racks are photography,
licensed characters and designer one-offs. Our differentiator is that
every card is ABOUT the person (their thing, their number, their
world) in a house style nobody can rack. £8.99 sells that, not paper.

## Tone menu (buyer-facing) — dial D3, recommended

**Funny / Warm / Cheeky**, with **Rude** behind the existing opt-in
toggle. Maps internally onto the three-angle machinery so every tone
still yields three genuinely different cards. Evidence: Thortful's
style aisles (Funny flagship, Cute/Pretty warm cluster, Cheeky and
Rude distinct); Scribbler proves the rude pole carries a whole brand.

## Age bands — three different worlds (evidence, not guesses)

- **18–25 · THRESHOLD.** The joke is adulthood itself ("childhood
  expired", "adulting", drink-sensibly irony) plus meme-fluency.
  Palette: loud, energetic, neon-adjacent brights on saturated
  grounds. Type: bold, sporty, playful. Note: at 18/21 the buyer is
  often a PARENT or GRANDPARENT — the rack needs warm variants beside
  the cheek, not cheek alone.
- **30–50 · KNOWING.** Self-deprecation with a straight face
  ("Thirties? Completed It Mate", "Act Like A Grown Up", "Forty and
  Fabulous"). Pop-culture era references begin (Ab Fab, Scrabble
  gags). Palette: confident, saturated, adult — the full Quirky range.
  Type: wit carried by typography; typeled shines here.
- **60+ · ERA & AFFECTION.** Nostalgia leads (born-in-1966, "Your
  Fifties Have Left The Building") with affectionate HABIT jokes (the
  power nap, the tea), never decline mockery. Palette: warm,
  golden-hour, vintage-leaning. Type: classic serifs, letterpress
  moods.

## Milestone machinery

- The NUMBER is artwork — already our trick, now the centre of every
  milestone rack.
- **The birth-year carve-out (needs careful prompt wording):** when
  the brief states a milestone ("60th"), the birth year is DERIVED
  (current year − age) and the born-in-[year] / "Road to X" genre is
  allowed. Derived arithmetic only, from the buyer's own words; the
  never-invent-a-date rule holds everywhere else.
- Dynamic-number templates: Moonpig lets buyers set the age on
  milestone cards. Our catalogue can match this later — a template
  whose number re-renders — but NOT in v1.

## Recipient seams (from the aisles)

- **Mum**: the family dynamic IS the material ("enough about my
  sibling", "favourite child") alongside florals — both lanes stocked.
- **Dad**: kit-and-hobby jokes, the shed, the chair (our home turf).
- **Grandparent-buyer** lane at young milestones; **colleague-safe**
  lane always.

## Composition mix per rack

Every tone rack carries: one typeled (text-only — bestseller-
validated), statements, heroes, one dense pattern/label. No rack of
one density.

## The dials, as recommended (Aidan rules on each)

- **D1 age-roast:** allow inside Funny by default, CALIBRATED BY BAND
  (threshold jokes at 18–25, knowing self-deprecation 30–50, era/habit
  affection 60+ — never decline mockery). Evidence upgraded this from
  "opt-in only": the affectionate roast is the market's mainstream.
- **D2 swearing:** hold the asterisk mask as house style; pun-masking
  ("ducking hell") also allowed. Unmasked sells top-three at every age
  on Thortful, so this is a brand choice we can revisit with sales
  data.
- **D4 milestone doors first:** ⏸ DEFERRED to WS7 (Aidan unsure, and it
  blocks nothing — door order is a landing-page decision, not a prompt
  one). Decide it when the catalogue has depth: whichever milestones
  Aidan has actually enjoyed making cards for are the ones with stock
  to sell, and that beats either of our guesses.
